The problem

Your safety team is reading files that were never going to pass

Most recruiters send everything and let you sort it out. Every unqualified application is your staff's time — pulling an MVR, reading an employment history, writing the rejection. The driver loses an hour too, and tells other drivers about it.

We screen first, against your rules

Send us your qualification criteria — the same document you'd hand a new recruiter. How it works

From your standards to a seated driver

You do two things: send us your criteria, and tell us when someone starts. We do the rest.

1

You send your qualification criteria

Whatever form it's already in. Points tables, lookback periods, endorsements, truck requirements, your document checklist. We turn it into rules our system screens against — no new process on your side.

2

We recruit and pre-screen

Our own marketing, our own site, in both languages. Every applicant is scored against your standards before we send them anywhere.

3

Qualified drivers reach your application

We hand them your application link with your source tracking intact, so attribution in your system stays clean. Then we chase them until it's submitted and they have a confirmation number.

4

We chase the paperwork and the clocks

Clearinghouse consent, the drug-test window, the inspection that has to be dated within 30 days, the lease form, every document on your list. Our system tracks each deadline per driver and follows up automatically until it's done.

5

You tell us they started

That's when we invoice. Not before.

Why it's faster

We run our own software, not a spreadsheet

Most small recruiting shops run on a shared inbox and a spreadsheet, which is why files go quiet for a week. Ours is purpose-built for this, and it's the reason we can promise speed and mean it.

Your rules, as data

Each carrier's criteria live as their own rule set — points thresholds, lookback windows, experience minimums, document checklist. Adding a carrier is configuration, not a rewrite, so we can be screening against your standards the same week you send them.

Nothing waits on someone remembering

Every driver's outstanding step has a deadline attached, and follow-ups fire on their own — twice a day, rate-limited so nobody gets hounded. A file stalls only if the driver has genuinely gone quiet, and then we call.

One packet, complete

When a driver's file is ready, you get it in one piece: their details, their lease information, and every document, checked against your list. If something's missing we know before you do.

Sensitive data handled properly

Social security numbers, licence numbers and bank details are encrypted at rest and destroyed once the lease is signed. We publish exactly what we hold and for how long.
